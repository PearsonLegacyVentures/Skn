What to expect

Yacht sessions require additional planning for a stable table, privacy, safe boarding and weather.

Plan your treatment space

Share the vessel name, captain approval, marina or anchorage and an authorised boarding contact.

Your location must be accessible and suitable for a private professional treatment. Hotels, yachts and villas may have their own requirements. St. Kitts and Nevis are separate islands, so staffing and travel are always checked individually.

Duration and final cost

Request 60 or 90 minutes and the number of guests. We confirm the full final quote after reviewing the property, access, staffing and transport. This page does not promise an unverified fixed island price, and the request form does not collect payment.

How to request yacht massage

  1. Choose your treatment and desired duration.
  2. Share the exact island, property and number of guests.
  3. Select a start at least six hours ahead in St. Kitts & Nevis local time.
  4. Wait for concierge confirmation of availability, arrangements and full price.
