Planning private massage in Cockleshell Bay

Cockleshell Bay is on the southeastern St. Kitts peninsula, away from Basseterre's city setting. Beach venues and resort or villa stays have distinct legal and practical conditions.

Accommodation access and treatment space

A guest should identify an actual property or authorised room. A beach bar, public shoreline or boat landing should not be treated as a permitted massage venue without express approval.

Services for your stay

An individual relaxation session or couples treatment may be requested in a suitably private and sheltered setting. Staffing and any travel charges must be confirmed for the south-coast location.

Arrival and island logistics

The peninsula's roads and property entrances matter, particularly for guests arriving by boat. A general Cockleshell Bay pin cannot replace a private treatment address.

Can treatment be set up directly on Cockleshell Beach?

Only if a specific lawful location permits it and the practitioner considers the environment private, safe and suitable.
