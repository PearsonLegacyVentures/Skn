BEFORE YOU REQUEST

Good to know.

This is a mobile service-area operation, not a walk-in spa on either island.

Is there a Tranquilitas walk-in spa in St. Kitts or Nevis?

No walk-in Tranquilitas location is advertised in either island. We arrange mobile treatments at suitable private properties when local coverage is confirmed.

How much notice is required?

At least six hours before the requested start, measured in St. Kitts & Nevis local time (UTC−4). More may be needed for travel, staffing or special setups.

Can you treat guests in a resort room?

Only when the property's guest policy permits outside practitioners and a safe private treatment space is available.

Can I request couples massage on Nevis?

Yes, as an enquiry. A simultaneous couples treatment requires two therapists and space for two tables, subject to local availability.

Massage and mobile spa questions in St. Kitts & Nevis

St. Kitts and Nevis are separate delivery zones even when the customer is staying in the same country.

Can I request massage at a Frigate Bay resort?

Private treatment enquiries are considered for eligible hotels and residences. The resort must allow external professionals and an appropriate local practitioner must be confirmed.

Frigate Bay massage

Are private massage appointments offered on Nevis?

A guest can enquire about a Nevis villa, hotel or guesthouse. St. Kitts therapist availability does not establish an available professional on Nevis; travel and venue rules are checked separately.

Nevis bookings

Can I arrange a couples massage on St. Kitts?

A simultaneous couples session requires an approved room, enough equipment space and confirmed qualified local staff. We establish the full quote and arrival plan before booking.

Couples massage

What about yacht massage at Christophe Harbour?

Onboard sessions require captain approval, an identified berth, safe boarding, equipment suitability and a locally available professional.
