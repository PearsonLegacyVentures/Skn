BOOKING QUESTIONS

Your questions, answered.

Know what to expect before arranging private wellness.

A clear plan before payment.

Is there a Tranquilitas walk-in spa in St. Kitts or Nevis?

No walk-in Tranquilitas location is advertised in either island. We arrange mobile treatments at suitable private properties when local coverage is confirmed.

How much notice is required?

At least six hours before the requested start, measured in St. Kitts & Nevis local time (UTC−4). More may be needed for travel, staffing or special setups.

Can you treat guests in a resort room?

Only when the property's guest policy permits outside practitioners and a safe private treatment space is available.

Can I request couples massage on Nevis?

Yes, as an enquiry. A simultaneous couples treatment requires two therapists and space for two tables, subject to local availability.

Are massage prices shown before I request a booking?

Yes. St. Kitts rates and a provisional service subtotal are shown for Swedish, deep tissue, couples and beach massage. Nevis and additional travel or setup costs are quoted separately. The form does not collect payment or secure a reservation.

Are St. Kitts and Nevis therapist schedules shared?

No. These are separate islands, and we cannot assume a therapist can travel between them for an immediate appointment.

What about yacht or beach treatments?

These require suitable conditions, permission, a safe table location and—in the case of yachts—captain or operator approval.
